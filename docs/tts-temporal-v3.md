# TTS Temporal V3

> Historical contract. Production has moved to [TTS Temporal V4](./tts-temporal-v4.md).

`TTS_TEMPORAL_V3` was the production dialogue-dubbing path used by the Transcript Editor and the durable `SYNTHESIZE_TTS` worker job.

## Authority

- Translation may use the configured LLM, in bounded dialogue batches.
- ASR, timeline repair, duration scoring, WAV measurement, fitting, assembly and mixing are local-first.
- Locked production voice: OmniVoice `k2-fsa/OmniVoice`, `instruct:vi_female_north`.
- The approved translation remains semantic authority. TTS never invents a replacement sentence.
- Numbers and units are protected while ranking saved translation candidates.
- Heavy full-video lip sync is out of scope. Most segments use scene/timeline alignment.

## Runtime flow

1. Resolve approved current translation rows and their transcript source, speaker and timing metadata.
2. Repair ASR micro-boundaries when adjacent rows have a safe gap and the same known speaker.
3. Allocate at most 320 ms of unused following pause while retaining an 80 ms inter-segment gap.
4. Calibrate Vietnamese spoken units per second from recent clips generated by the same provider, voice and rate.
5. Rank the approved current text plus saved duration candidates locally. Candidates that lose protected numbers or units are rejected.
6. Synthesize the best predicted candidate, trim provider-only edge silence and measure the real WAV duration.
7. Regenerate only a blocked segment at a bounded voice rate (maximum 1.25) and apply pitch-preserving `atempo` only up to 1.15.
8. Assemble a full-duration narration track. Subtitle cues are built from this final synthesized timeline.
9. Preserve the separated original music/SFX/ambient stem in the render-prep manifest.
10. Persist temporal QA artifacts and expose a summary in the frontend Transcript Editor.

## Performance and durability

- Exact fitted segment WAVs are cached by pipeline version, text, duration budget, provider/model, voice and runtime recipe authority.
- Cache writes use WAV first and JSON as the completion marker; invalid/incomplete entries are ignored.
- Translation and local OmniVoice jobs are limited to one running job per workspace to avoid duplicate provider calls and GPU/VRAM contention.
- OmniVoice batching is hardware-aware: batch 1 below 6 GB VRAM, batch 2 from 6 GB and batch 4 from 10 GB. This avoids the autoregressive batching slowdown measured on 4 GB cards.
- A retry resumes through the durable worker contract and reuses valid segment cache entries.

## Frontend contract

`Generate TTS` saves/approves the current Vietnamese draft, creates or follows the durable TTS job, and reloads `/source-videos/{id}/tts-summary`. The report shows pipeline version, repaired groups, probes, selective corrections, background preservation and final timing status.
